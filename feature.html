01 — The Paradigm Shift

A New Architecture

The 2025/2026 season marks a seismic shift. Following the historic election of André Villas-Boas—ending a 42-year presidency—the club embarked on aggressive modernization. At the center stands Francesco Farioli. At 36, the Italian tactician represents the antithesis of the old regime—a philosopher tasked with marrying historical dominance with a modern aesthetic.

Fig II. The Architect Francesco Farioli
Photo: Miguel Pereira / DCP
02 — Fariolismo

Control & Chaos

The tactical identity is built on nerve. Farioli’s Porto seeks to hold the ball in deep zones, baiting the opponent to press before exploiting the space behind. It is a high-wire act that has yielded the best defensive record in the country. With 65% average possession, they do not just win; they suffocate.

Fig III. The Press High Line Recovery
Photo: Sarah Jenkins / Getty
03 — The Spearhead

Ruthless Efficiency

At the tip of the spear is Samu Aghehowa. The signing has been a masterstroke, with the Spaniard netting 19 goals in all competitions. While the build-up is complex, the outcome is simple. He provides the physical presence that allows the creators to flourish in the pockets of space created by the system.

Fig IV. The Release Samu Aghehowa (Goal 19)
Photo: Diogo Cardoso / DCP
04 — The Wall

The Sweeper-Keeper

At the other end, Diogo Costa has evolved. No longer just a shot-stopper, he operates as an eleventh outfield player, sweeping outside his box to sustain the high defensive line. His distribution is the first pass of every attack, and his 13 clean sheets are the foundation upon which this renaissance is built.

Fig V. The Wall Defensive Organization
Photo: DCP Archive
05 — La Famiglia Portista

Gravitational Center

When the net ripples, the tactical geometry dissolves. The goal celebrations in this era are distinct—magnetic collisions of bodies that gravitate toward the touchline. Farioli is rarely a distant observer; he is often engulfed in the collective. This physical connection demonstrates that wins are not achieved by instruction alone, but by a unified pulse.
