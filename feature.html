01 — The Paradigm Shift

The End of the Monolith

The 2025/2026 season marks a seismic shift. Following the historic election of André Villas-Boas—ending a 42-year presidency—the club embarked on aggressive modernization.

Francesco Farioli, 36, is the visual representation of this new era. A disciple of the aesthetic school, his mandate was to marry Porto's historical dominance with a sophisticated brand of football capable of valorizing assets in a volatile market.

03 — The Spearhead

Ruthless Efficiency

At the tip of the spear is Samu Aghehowa. The signing has been a masterstroke, with the Spaniard netting 19 goals in all competitions.

While the build-up is complex, the outcome is simple. Samu provides the physical presence to occupy center-backs, allowing creators like Gabri Veiga to flourish in the pockets of space created by the system.
